# On-Device 'Voz' Podcast Transcription & Auto-Skip Plan

Integrate Desert Ant's lightweight, on-device **Voz** speech-to-text model to locally transcribe podcast audio, extract word-level timestamps, detect sponsored/promotional segments, and visually render and auto-jump over ad segments in the podcast player.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The choices below reflect your confirmed preferences from Phase 1 interactive clarification:

- **Target Segments to Skip**: **Sponsored ads and promotional segments only**. Other content (intros, outros, or silence) will remain unskipped.
- **Model Execution Strategy**: **Offline On-Device 'Voz' STT**. Processing is performed locally on-device without cloud API calls or external server audio uploads.
- **Player Auto-Skip Experience**: **Visual Progress Bar Markers + Seamless Auto-Jump**. Ad segments are marked with subtle highlight bars on the scrubber, and playback automatically jumps past the segment when reached (if auto-skip is enabled), without annoying toast popups or intrusive overlays.

---

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Transcribes podcast episodes on-device using the `Voz` local speech-to-text engine. The transcription engine identifies timestamped sentences, analyzes key promotional indicators (e.g. "sponsored by", "discount code", "promo code", "ad segment"), flags sponsor bounds, and allows the playback engine to jump over those timestamps seamlessly.
- **Target Audience**: Listeners who want uninterrupted podcast streams without manual scrubbing through host-read promotional spots or sponsor announcements.
- **Key Value**: 100% private, offline transcription with zero per-minute audio streaming API fees and real-time visual ad indicator markers on the progress scrubber.

---

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

- **Player View & Progress Scrubber**:
  - **Sponsor Segment Markers**: Distinct highlight regions drawn along the `Slider` / progress track corresponding to detected promotional start and end timestamps.
  - **Auto-Jump Toggle**: Clean icon button in the player control bar (e.g., `Shuttle / FastForward` with indicator badge) to toggle "Auto-Skip Ads" ON or OFF.
  - **Seamless Jump Animation**: When the playback head enters an ad segment (and auto-skip is active), the player cleanly jumps the `ExoPlayer` / `Media3` position to the end timestamp of the ad segment smoothly.
- **Transcript & Skip Inspector Sheet**:
  - Bottom sheet or expandable drawer displaying the full episode text transcript generated by `Voz` with timecode badges.
  - Highlighted ad blocks in the transcript with manual "Jump to Segment" or "Exclude from Skip" toggles.

---

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: On-Device STT Engine (`Voz` Parakeet/TDT Integration)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Lightweight Kotlin / ONNX-based `Voz` engine integration with fallback keyword-timestamp pattern matcher for local segment detection.
  - *Why*: Ensures complete offline operation without sending audio streams to cloud LLM providers, matching the explicit user requirement.
  - *Trade-Off*: Initial model weight cache (~450MB model asset) vs. unlimited zero-cost on-device transcription.

- **Decision 2: Automated Segment Detection Pipeline**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Stream-chunked audio analysis yielding timestamped transcript segments, evaluated against rule-based sponsor trigger heuristics (and local lightweight LLM phrase pattern matching).
  - *Why*: Delivers low-latency detection of common sponsor markers ("use code...", "this episode is brought to you by...", "thanks to our sponsor...") right after buffering audio.

### Component & Data Model Flow

1. **`VozTranscriber`**:
   - Accepts episode audio URI/file.
   - Outputs `TranscriptResult(sentences: List<SentenceTimestamp>)`.
2. **`SponsorSegmentDetector`**:
   - Analyzes sentences for promotional language and returns `List<SkipRange(startMs, endMs, label)>`.
3. **`PodcastPlayerViewModel`**:
   - Maintains `skipRanges`, `isAutoSkipEnabled`, and monitors playback position updates.
   - When `currentPositionMs` enters `skipRange.startMs` and `isAutoSkipEnabled` is true, calls `player.seekTo(skipRange.endMs)`.
4. **`PlayerView` Scrubber Canvas**:
   - Draws custom colored highlight lines over the seek track corresponding to `skipRanges`.
